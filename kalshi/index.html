Leverage sets the distance. At 5x with 50% maintenance, a 10% move against you is liquidation. At 2x it's 25%. The dial you choose is the risk you carry.
Liquidation is not a stop loss. It's forced closure at the worst moment, and in fast markets execution isn't guaranteed at the trigger. Set your own exits first.
The Kalshi angle. Single-digit leverage caps mean the cushion starts wider than offshore's 50–100x, where a 1–2% wick is fatal. That's a feature. Teach it that way.

Premium: longs pay shorts. When the perp trades above the index the crowd is leaning long, and funding charges them to pull it back down.
Discount: shorts pay longs. Below the index, shorts pay. Funding is the market paying you to take the unpopular side.
Read it as a signal. Persistent positive funding means crowded longs. That's module 4: funding as sentiment, not just a fee.
The cap is the story. Drag past ±2.00% and the rate stops moving while the dislocation keeps growing. Offshore, that gap keeps billing you. Kalshi caps it. Hardest thing to teach in a paragraph, easiest to feel on a slider.
