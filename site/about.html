About SEMC

Built by people who know what the OR demands.

Since 2009, Southeastern Medical Concepts has delivered superior results with premium products — one surgeon relationship at a time.

Our Approach

Equipment is easy. Support is the job.

At Southeastern Medical Concepts, we take pride in equipping our surgeons with cutting-edge products in the field of surgery.

But a catalog has never finished a case. Our consultants provide state-of-the-art support to clinicians using SEMC products — they know the instrumentation, they know the workflow, and they show up. That's what turns a vendor into a partner, and it's why surgeons who start with SEMC stay with SEMC.

From our base in South Florida we serve the Southeastern United States, with distribution reaching South and Central America.
